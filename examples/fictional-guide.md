## Input brief

A fictional community repair workshop wants a short guide to writing a reusable repair checklist. Reader: a volunteer drafting the guide. Goal: separate general preparation from item-specific instructions. Tone: practical, no customer story or effectiveness metric. No external research or website access requested.

## EN candidate — example v1

# A repair checklist needs a clear scope

A checklist is easier to use when it says which task it covers. A general preparation list can help a volunteer gather materials, but it should not pretend to explain every repair.

For this fictional workshop, separate the guide into two parts:

- Preparation: inspect the item, identify the material, and gather suitable tools.
- Task instructions: describe the specific repair, including any conditions that require experienced help.

Before reusing the checklist, compare it with the item in front of you. If a condition falls outside the guide, mark the gap rather than treating the checklist as complete.

This is a proposed document structure, not evidence that a workshop has improved its repair results.

## ZH candidate — example v1

# 修补清单要先写清适用范围

清单先说清楚要完成什么任务，才方便使用。通用准备清单可以帮助志愿者整理材料，但不能代替每一种修补方法的具体说明。

在这个虚构工作坊的示例里，可以把指南分成两部分：

- 准备工作：检查物品、辨认材料，备好适用工具。
- 具体操作：说明这次修补怎么做，以及什么情况下需要有经验的人协助。

再次使用清单前，先对照眼前的物品检查一遍。遇到指南没有覆盖的情况，就把缺口标出来，不要默认清单已经完整。

这只是文档结构建议，不代表某个工作坊已经因此改善了修补效果。
